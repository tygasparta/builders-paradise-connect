Final Requirement

This must look like premium commercial ERP software that businesses would confidently pay for. Every screen should be polished, consistent, and production-ready. Follow the inventory and accounting workflows defined in the supplied requirements document, including automated journal entries, GRN processing, stock adjustments, stock requisitions, POS rules, and reporting expectations.

Do not generate placeholders, dummy modules, or incomplete pages. Build every module end-to-end with a professional UI, complete validation, business logic, and seamless integration across the entire system.
